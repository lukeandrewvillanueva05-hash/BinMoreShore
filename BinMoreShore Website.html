ABOUT OUR PROJECT

What is BinMoreShore?

A community-focused environmental project designed for cleaner and more sustainable shores.

BinMoreShore is a proposed environmental solution that aims to address waste-management concerns in coastal communities.

Our project focuses on problems such as improper waste disposal, used diapers, broken or blocked drainage systems, unpleasant odors, and the accumulation of waste near coastal areas.

Rather than simply identifying the problem, BinMoreShore promotes awareness, proper waste management, community participation, and environmental responsibility.

The project is focused on Isla Bonita, Rosario, Cavite, where maintaining a clean coastal environment can benefit both the community and its surroundings.

THE PROBLEM

What Are We Addressing?

🗑️

Improper Waste Disposal

Improper disposal can cause waste to accumulate in communities and eventually reach waterways.

🚼

Used Diapers

Improperly discarded used diapers can contribute to waste-management and sanitation concerns.

🚧

Drainage Problems

Blocked or damaged drainage can affect water flow and contribute to stagnant water and flooding.

🌫️

Unpleasant Odor

Accumulated waste and stagnant water can create unpleasant smells in surrounding areas.

🌊

Coastal Pollution

Waste that reaches waterways can contribute to pollution and affect coastal cleanliness.

♻️

Poor Waste Management

Limited awareness of proper waste segregation and disposal can make environmental problems harder to manage.

WHY OUR SHORES MATTER

Protecting the Shore Starts With Us.

The shoreline is part of our community. Keeping it clean requires responsible waste management, awareness, and cooperation. BinMoreShore aims to encourage people to turn environmental awareness into action.
